# Notebook

Write on pages while a conversation records, on the open iPhone Duo or on one page, with the recorder as a small card in the corner. What you draw is kept as drawn, colours included, and read for words once you've finished.

- **Open it.** On the open Duo, Notebook (beside the camera at the top of the recorder) opens two facing pages split at the crease, nothing across it, and nothing at the far right where the status bar is. The display is the notebook: nothing is reserved above, below, or beside the pages, so each page takes the largest size that fits its half (about 0.875 on the open Duo, one scale for both) and sits against the crease with 16 pt clear. Folded shut with the notebook open it's one page, the width of the cover (about 0.87), and opening the Duo again returns the two. Partly open on a table, table mode takes over, and the pages come back when it's flat again. Any display too narrow for two pages at 0.7 shows one: the app decides by the space it has, not by the device.
- **Write.** Apple Pencil writes with nothing to switch on; until it's touched a page, a finger writes too (the pen's first touch turns fingers off, so a resting palm doesn't mark the page). The pen comes in black, blue, red, green, and orange, with a highlighter, an eraser, a lasso, and Undo and Redo. Pages are paper to their edges: ruled by default, always light, so the colours you wrote are the colours you see in Dark Mode too.
- **The tools and the pill float.** Both are glass over the top of the pages, and collapse so the paper under them can be written on. The tools sit top-left of the left page (the foot of a single page): Notes and the pen with its colours, or, short of room, a menu beside Undo and Redo. The pill sits top-right of the right page (the top of a single page), on one row: the phase, the clock, a small meter, Bookmark and Stop (Resume while paused), and the pages ("3–4 of 7", Previous, Next, New Page). Short of room it drops the meter, and at the larger text sizes puts the page controls on a second row. Tapping the clock closes the notebook back to the recorder. Either one collapses to a chip (the dot and clock; the tool and its colour) when dragged toward the display's edge, and by itself when a pen writes within about 40 pt of it, coming back a moment after the stroke ends (a fade under Reduce Motion, never a slide). Tapping the chip opens it. Stop settles the pages first, then files them with the conversation.
- **Pages.** A new page is made when something is written on it; a blank page is never kept. Deleting a page is undoable. Page turns are logged against the recording clock, so playback can later show the page that was open.
- **Notes.** The notes button opens the typed notes as a card over the right page, or a sheet when only one page shows. Typing and writing both belong to the conversation, and they don't mix: the notes stay the notes, and pages stay pages.
- **Under the pages.** A page's number sits in the strip under it (a menu: Words, Delete Page), and with live captions the newest line beside the last page's number; tap it to hide it, and its glyph to show it again. Where a page leaves no strip (a short display) the number is in the pill only and the caption floats as glass, stepping aside for a pen. A page's bottom outer corner turns it too.
- **Words.** Strokes are read when you've finished with them: at a page turn, at Stop, eight seconds after the last stroke, on leaving the notebook, when the layout changes, and when the app goes to the background; never while a stroke is being written. Strokes close in time and place are one passage (`PassageGrouping`), each read on its own by PencilKit's `PKStrokeRecognizer` on iOS 27 and by Apple Vision elsewhere, entirely on the device, and timed from its first stroke through the recorder's clock, so it lines up with what was said. A passage with few words in it is a sketch. A page's Words lists its passages with their times; a passage's words can be corrected there, and the correction stands while its strokes do.
- **Erasures.** Strokes you take out are noted (which stroke, when it was made, when it went and where in the recording), up to 200 a page, because PencilKit keeps only the strokes a drawing has now. Nothing reads the log yet.
- **A page whose strokes aren't on this iPhone.** Opened in a recording that continues a conversation, the page shows as loading and can't be written on until its strokes arrive; if they can't be had it says so ("This page’s writing isn’t available.") and stays shut. It's never opened blank over writing that exists, and nothing is ever saved over it.
- **Privacy.** Strokes, recognition, and the readings stay on the device; the words reach summaries only as notes do (docs/cloud.md). Pages travel like photos, through your iCloud (docs/icloud-sync.md).

Debug launches: `--start-recording --synthetic-audio --notebook --demo-pages` opens the notebook over three synthetic pages (writing in several colours, a sketch, a corrected passage); `--notebook-page <n>` and `--notebook-panel notes|words` open at a page or with a card showing.
