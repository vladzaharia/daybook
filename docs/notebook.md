# Notebook

Write on pages while a conversation records, on the open iPhone Duo or on one page, with the recorder as a small card in the corner. What you draw is kept as drawn, colours included, and read for words once you've finished.

- **Open it.** On the open Duo, Notebook (beside the camera at the top of the recorder) opens two facing pages split at the crease, nothing across it, and nothing at the far right where the status bar is. The display is the notebook: nothing is kept beside the pages, and above them only the band the controls use when it fits, so each page takes the largest size that fits its half (about 0.875 on the open Duo, one scale for both) and sits against the crease with 16 pt clear. Folded shut with the notebook open it's one page, the width of the cover (about 0.87), and opening the Duo again returns the two. Partly open on a table, table mode takes over, and the pages come back when it's flat again. Any display too narrow for two pages at 0.7 shows one: the app decides by the space it has, not by the device.
- **Write.** Apple Pencil writes with nothing to switch on; until it's touched a page, a finger writes too (the pen's first touch turns fingers off, so a resting palm doesn't mark the page). The pen comes in black, blue, red, green, and orange, with a highlighter, an eraser, a lasso, and Undo and Redo. Pages are paper to their edges: ruled by default, always light, so the colours you wrote are the colours you see in Dark Mode too.
- **The tools and the pill float.** Both are glass, and where they sit depends on the room outside the paper. The tools are Notes and the pen with its colours (or, short of room, a menu beside Undo and Redo); the pill is on one row: the phase, the clock, a small meter, Bookmark and Stop (Resume while paused), then the pages ("3–4 of 7", Previous, Next, New Page). Every control in the row is 44 pt high, Stop a red capsule with the stop glyph. Short of room the pill drops the meter, and at the larger text sizes (and on a Duo's page, from the first larger size) puts the page controls on a second row. Tapping the clock closes the notebook back to the recorder.
  - **Expanded in a band above the pages** when there is room: pages are bound by width, so on the open Duo (safe area 867 x 635, crease at 475.5) they are drawn on their caption strips instead of centred, which frees a band 80 pt tall above them (635 - 34 strip - 520.6 page) without changing the page size. The tools sit over the left page and the pill over the right, 4 pt in from the top and clear of the paper. The band must hold the pill with 4 pt above and below (60 pt one row; 104 pt two rows, which the Duo's band does not hold at the larger text sizes). A single page needs the tools above the pill as well (108 pt).
  - **As chips in the band** where it holds a row of chips (52 pt) but not the expanded controls (the cover's band is 92 pt; the Duo's at larger text sizes): the tool and its colour at the left and the dot and clock at the right, above the page, so while collapsed none of the paper is covered. A tap expands one over the top of the page (the pill under the tools on a single page); it collapses again when a pen comes within about 40 pt or writing starts, and stays collapsed until tapped (a fade under Reduce Motion, never a slide). Only a display with no room above the page at all keeps the chips over its top margin.
  - The ruling does not move: the first rule stays two lines (56 pt) down and the lines 28 pt apart, so pages already written keep their strokes on their rules. In the band a control also collapses to its chip when dragged toward the display's edge. The notes card starts at the top of the right page unless the controls float over it. Stop settles the pages first, then files them with the conversation.
- **Pages.** A new page is made when something is written on it; a blank page is never kept. Deleting a page is undoable. Page turns are logged against the recording clock, so playback can later show the page that was open.
- **Notes.** The notes button opens the typed notes as a card over the right page, or a sheet when only one page shows. Typing and writing both belong to the conversation, and they don't mix: the notes stay the notes, and pages stay pages.
- **Under the pages.** A page's number sits in the strip under it (a menu: Words, Leave Out of Summaries or Include in Summaries, Delete Page; a page left out shows a small mark beside its number, and is skipped by summaries and by sketch reading, while recording and in a saved conversation's notebook), and with live captions the newest line beside the last page's number; tap it to hide it, and its glyph to show it again. Where a page leaves no strip (a short display) the number is in the pill only and the caption floats as glass, stepping aside for a pen. A page's bottom outer corner turns it too.
- **Words.** Strokes are read when you've finished with them: at a page turn, at Stop, eight seconds after the last stroke, on leaving the notebook, when the layout changes, and when the app goes to the background; never while a stroke is being written. Strokes close in time and place are one passage (`PassageGrouping`), each read on its own by PencilKit's `PKStrokeRecognizer` on iOS 27 and by Apple Vision elsewhere, entirely on the device, and timed from its first stroke through the recorder's clock, so it lines up with what was said. A passage with few words in it is a sketch. A page's Words lists its passages with their times; a passage's words can be corrected there, and the correction stands while its strokes do.
- **Erasures.** Strokes you take out are noted (which stroke, when it was made, when it went and where in the recording), up to 200 a page, because PencilKit keeps only the strokes a drawing has now. Nothing reads the log yet.
- **A page whose strokes aren't on this iPhone.** Opened in a recording that continues a conversation, the page shows as loading and can't be written on until its strokes arrive; if they can't be had it says so ("This page’s writing isn’t available.") and stays shut. It's never opened blank over writing that exists, and nothing is ever saved over it.
- **A saved conversation's notebook.** More › Notebook opens its pages full screen in any posture: two pages split at the crease on the open Duo, one page on the cover, with Done in the pill's place and the pages controls beside it. The tools, the notes card (the conversation's own notes), and Words work as while recording. Strokes are committed as while recording (settle points, and a draft file every moment so a crash loses little), and a page changed on this iPhone merges with changes made elsewhere like any other part of the conversation. While a page is open, a page nobody has written on follows its strokes when others arrive by sync; a page written on keeps what it has, and its commit joins the strokes that arrived (by stroke, in the order they were made) except ones erased here since, and is refused, with the draft kept, when the stored strokes can't be had. The draft file stays until the conversation has been saved to the disk, and after a crash it is joined with the page the same way: a draft whose page was deleted goes, a missing page is brought back with it, and one whose page holds strokes not on this iPhone waits for them.
- **Playing from the handwriting.** In Words, a passage's time is a button that plays the conversation from when it was written; holding a finger on handwriting does the same once Apple Pencil has been used (until then a finger writes). A passage timed inside a pause starts at the end of the silence the pause left, not in it. Only saved conversations play: while recording nothing does.
- **Writing while it plays.** Strokes written in a saved conversation's notebook while it plays take the player's position on the loaded recording (the recording and seconds into it) as their time, rather than the wall clock (sampled when the notebook opens, when a stroke begins, and whenever the player plays, pauses, seeks, changes speed, or changes recording, the sample after a stroke standing for it as the recorder's do); not playing, only the wall clock is kept. A passage written during a recording this conversation was filed with is placed from that recording's filed clock. Page turns made while playing are logged against that clock too.
- **Strokes not on this iPhone.** Opened, a page whose strokes haven't downloaded shows as loading, and can't be written on until they arrive (they're fetched on open); if they can't be had it says "This page’s writing isn’t available." and stays shut, and tries again when the page is shown again or the page's strokes change. A page is never opened blank over strokes that exist, and nothing is saved over one.
- **In summaries.** A page's writing is a note: each writing passage of a page that isn't deleted or taken out of summaries, with its corrected words in place of what was read, is a timed note labelled H1, H2… in time order, at the moment it was written (`NOTE H1, handwritten on page 2 by the user (not spoken; read from handwriting, so a word may be misread)`), with the weight any note has; passages with no time come after the timed ones, in page order, as notes written outside the recording. Words a typed note already has (whole words) are left out. Only a reading made from the page's current drawing counts, and only a passage kind this version knows: a page not read yet, one read from older strokes, and a passage of a kind a newer Daybook wrote are not summary input, so erased words never reach a summary. An iPhone reads the pages that need it before it summarizes, and a Mac describes the sketches that need it (the Mac does not read handwriting yet; it uses the words an iPhone read). A summary keeps a short digest of the notebook input it was written from (`MeetingSummary.notebookDigest`, a digest of `Meeting.notebookSummaryInputs`), and it travels with the summary through sync. When a reading arrives later (the iPhone files and reads after a Mac summarized, or a sketch's drawing reaches the Mac after), or a passage is corrected or a page hidden, the conversation's notebook input differs from the digest and the summary's foot says "Notebook changed" beside its date (`Meeting.summaryNotebookChanged`); Summarize Again takes the pages in and clears it. Nothing is re-run on its own, so no placement or cloud rule is bypassed, and a run that again finds no reading stores the same digest and does not ask again. A summary written before the digest was kept has none and is never marked. A reading that lands while a summary is being written is written again, as for any changed input. A sketch is picture evidence, S1, S2…: its strokes are drawn dark on light paper, read on the device the way a photo is (text, what it shows, a caption), on an iPhone before it summarizes and on a Mac before it summarizes (the picture is rendered the same on both, `SketchReading`, and Vision runs on a queue of its own; a page whose drawing hasn't reached the Mac is skipped, not failed, and read once it does), and the reading is kept with the page as long as its strokes are the same; a summary can place a sketch under a section, as it does a photo (`SummarySection.sketchIDs`). In a long conversation each part gets the handwriting, sketches and photos whose time falls in it; one with no time reaches only the final write-up. A conversation with notes or pages and no speech is summarized from them, and the source says there was no speech (a bookmark's name alone is not content); Summarize Again and a template change are offered for it as for any conversation with something to summarize (`Meeting.hasSummarizableContent`). The note payload the model receives marks handwriting with its page. A correction that changes what a page says makes the summary out of date, as a note edit does; reading the same strokes again doesn't.
- **Search and exports.** Library search finds a conversation by the words on its pages (corrections included, deleted pages not) and says the match is in the Notebook. Markdown exports have a Notebook part with each page's words in order and a sketch's description; the full-data export carries each page's drawing file, its reading, and the recordings' clock samples.
- **Privacy.** Strokes, recognition, and the readings stay on the device; the words reach summaries only as notes do (docs/cloud.md), and so only under the same placement and policy rules. A page's picture, a sketch, its description and its label never go to a cloud model; a cloud summary places sketches by time afterwards, from what was read on the device. Pages travel like photos, through your iCloud (docs/icloud-sync.md).

Debug launches: `--start-recording --synthetic-audio --notebook --demo-pages` opens the notebook over three synthetic pages (writing in several colours, a sketch, a corrected passage); `--notebook-page <n>` and `--notebook-panel notes|words` open at a page or with a card showing.
