## Notes on partial fixes

- **cross-device-consistency-rd-01:** Linking, facades, UIInk from DaybookShade, the shared DaybookLinkStyle, TimeLabel removal, and a parity test are all done. Not done: replacing LevelHistory with LevelTrail and RollingMeter with RollingLevelMeter. Those are iPhone recording files outside my paths, for wave 2.
- **cross-device-consistency-rd-04:** Added DaybookTile, FigureTile (the Mac's richer tile with a detail line), StatusCard, StatusRow, StatusDivider, and AttentionBox with a primary, secondary, and dismiss form that follows the Mac's rules. Not done: deleting the Mac's DeviceBox and DeviceGlyph and the iPhone's StatusTile, DeviceGlyph, and ProblemBox. Those are wave 2.
- **cross-device-consistency-rd-08:** Every live-caption view in my paths now renders through LiveCaptionStrip: Floating Notes, the iPhone recording screen (.strip/.page/.stage), the Mac Listen Live page (new .feed preset: 6 lines, body type, follows reading scale, glyph column shared with the Listening row) and the Mac page section while recording. The strip scales with Dynamic Type under touch, its label is always on top, and there is a new .sheet preset (3 lines). LiveCaptionStatus, LiveCaptionLabels (lane/speaker) and LiveCaptionTurn moved to DaybookCaptions. DaybookUI adds CaptionLine(segment, names:) and LiveCaptionStrip(segments:state:locale:names:style:) / .shows. The iPhone's LiveCaptionsView and the Mac's LiveCaptionFeed stay only as small adapters, so the RecordingSurface.swift and DeviceRecordingPages.swift call sites are untouched. Still left: the iPhone status sheet's StatusCaptions (w2-status-sheet's file) still draws its own captions; that package needs to switch it to LiveCaptionStrip(segments:state:locale:style: .sheet).
- **cross-device-consistency-rd-14:** Done: the shared DeviceRecordingsView (Mac list column, iPhone 'All Recordings' push) and one DeviceRecordingActions list: Import, Add to “overlap”, Choose Conversation…, Remove from Queue, Stop Importing, Show Conversation, and Show in Finder on the Mac. ConversationChooser is a shared sheet with search. Left out on purpose: 'Import Again as New Conversation'. The session and both importers check the recording's identity right before ingesting, so the old menu item transferred the whole recording and then threw it away. Making it work would need DaybookDevices and importer changes.
