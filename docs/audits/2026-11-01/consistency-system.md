- **xdevice-ds-20:** Scaffolding only, as planned: DaybookUI holds ReadingGeometry, ReadingPage, MarginRow, PageSpread and PageSpreadLayout, SurfaceLayout, PlatformMetrics, daybookSurface, ReadingContext, ReadingNavigator, the ReadingPlayer protocol, ReadingAudio, and ScreenServices. Not done: moving ReadingSession, moving the Markdown parser into DaybookKit (not my path), and moving the screens themselves. Those are wave 2.
- **xdevice-ds-10:** Added ProblemLine, AttentionBox(kind: .problem/.accent/.info, title, symbol, message, compact, actions, plus a primary/secondary/dismiss form), and DetailsButton to DaybookDesign. DetailsButton opens a popover on the Mac and a sheet on iOS, or goes through detailsPresenter / ScreenServices.presentDetails for the Duo's page sheet. Its label is "Details…" on the Mac and "Details" on the iPhone, following the owner's recorded choice in docs/copy-pass-2026-10.md:164. Not done: replacing ProblemText, the cloud-key status, MacsSettings, ProblemBox, NoticeRow, and the photo line. Those files belong to wave-2 owners.
- **xdevice-ds-11:** (b) is fixed: the iPhone now uses the shared DaybookLinkStyle, which dims to 45% when disabled. (d) is provided: .daybookTinted (onWash ink on a 14% wash) and .daybookProminent. Not done: (a), the empty-library Record (LibraryView.swift), and (c), the amber bookmark in the Mac player (MeetingDetailView.swift). Both are call sites outside my paths, for wave 2; RecordControlLabel already exists for (a).
- **xdevice-ds-12:** A typed tag is normalized once, in DaybookKit's TagInput and Meeting.tags(adding:): it splits at commas, trims, collapses runs of spaces, drops a leading '#', and skips repeats regardless of case. There's one TagEditor with DaybookChip's .tag colours. The Mac shows the × on hover; the iPhone always shows it, with a 44 pt hit target. Info's tag field gives 'client, beta' two tags on both apps. Still to do: the iPhone's Tags sheet (LibrarySheets.swift TagsSheet.addNew, owned by w2-library-list) still swaps commas for spaces. It should call TagInput.adding(draft, to: tags). The other chip copies (OwnerChip, ModelLabelChip, and so on) belong to other packages.
